2 Related Work
Quantitative Earnings Prediction: The PEAD
Literature.The PEAD phenomenon—first iden-
tified by Ball and Brown (1968) and formalized
by Bernard and Thomas (1989)—documents that
stock returns drift in the direction of earnings
surprise for days to weeks after announcement,
measurable via standardized unexpected earnings
(SUE) and evaluated with quintile spread (Q5−Q1)
and cumulative abnormal return (CAR) (Latane
and Jones, 1979). Revenue surprises carry incre-
mental predictive content beyond EPS (Jegadeesh
and Livnat, 2006), and management guidance
moves prices independently at announcement time
(Billings et al., 2015). Critically, Martineau (2022)
show that multi-day PEAD has been non-existent
for non-microcap stocks since 2006, prices now
fully reflect earnings surprises on the announce-
ment date itself, as near-instantaneous price jumps
replace gradual drift (Christensen et al., 2025).
This compression of the exploitable window moti-
vates our evalua

tion at short holding periods: quan-
titative surprise alpha has not disappeared, it has
accelerated, and this work recovers it by evaluating
with immediate actions rather than waiting for the
next market open.
Qualitative Earnings Prediction: NLP and ECT-
Based Models.The dominant NLP paradigm
applies multimodal learning over ECT audio and
text (Qin and Yang, 2019; Yang et al., 2020; Cao
et al., 2024, 2025), with extensions for numeri-
cal reasoning (Yang et al., 2022; Qin et al., 2025),
graph-based inter-stock modeling (Sawhney et al.,
2020; Liu et al., 2024), and agentic workflows (Li
and Lu, 2026). Recent work questions the infor-
mativeness of ECT embeddings as a standalone
signal: same-company transcripts are highly for-
mulaic across periods, limiting incremental infor-
mation beyond boilerplate (Shih et al., 2025; Yu
et al., 2025). Meursault et al. (2023) show that text-
based ECT patterns produce drift larger than clas-
sic PEAD, providing independent support for our
fast/slow distinction — yet all these works evaluate
at the multi-day horizon where drift has largely dis-
appeared (see above) under misaligned pointwise
metrics; §3.2 elaborates. Instead, this work applies
LLM sentiment scoring over ECTs and news under
a long/short trading framework.

magnitude) and are all reflected in financial news
articles; while stage (4) carries primarilyqualita-
tiveinformation recorded as earnings conference

bility signals, and forward guidance language. The
two are structurally linked: the ECT is alinguistic

numbers have been public for 30–90 minutes, with
management exercisingdiscretionary framingover
how results are presented: a company can beat EPS

confidence, language and numbers can tell entirely
opposite stories.

complementarity(numbers encodewhatwas re-

through two lenses: thefinancial economics com-

return; theNLP communityfocuses on ECT-based

5.2 交易机制
遵循经典交易设置，两种策略类型均在每个交易时段内做多排名前10%的信号并做空排名后10%的信号。它们的区别在于排名的形成方式：
• 经典PEAD（Bernard和Thomas，1989）：在每个交易时段内进行同期横截面排序；适用于NextOpen（次日开盘），此时所有公告均已知（例如，10只股票在同一天发布报告：买入超出预期最多的股票，卖出不及预期最严重的股票）。
• 历史百分位：将每个信号与过去12个月的扩展窗口累积分布函数（CDF）进行对比排名。这是FirstActual（首次实际值）入场所必需的，因为股票在整个交易时段内都会发布财报，使得同期排序不可行（例如，一只股票的得分处于整个市场过去12个月信号分布的前10%，则买入该股票）。
