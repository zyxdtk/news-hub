摘要

在有限的密度评估下从未归一化的多模态分布中采样，仍然是机器学习和自然科学领域的一个根本挑战。
成功的方法在易于处理的参考分布和目标分布之间构建了一座桥梁。
并行回火（PT）作为黄金标准，而近期基于扩散的方法提供了一种连续的替代方案，但代价是需要进行神经网络训练。
在这项工作中，我们引入了条件扩散采样（CDS），这是一个结合了这两种范式的框架。
为此，我们推导了条件插值器（Conditional Interpolants），这是一类随机过程，其传输动力学由精确的闭式随机微分方程（SDE）控制，无需神经近似。
尽管这些动力学需要从非平凡的初始化分布中采样，但我们从理论和实验上证明，对于足够短的扩散时间，这种初始化的代价会逐渐减小。
CDS通过一个两阶段过程利用了这一点：（1）使用PT高效地采样初始分布，然后（2）通过传输SDE对样本进行传输。
这种结合将PT鲁棒的全局探索与高效的局部传输结合在一起。
实验表明，CDS有潜力在样本质量

and density evaluation cost compared to state-of-the-art samplers.

4.1
Annealing-based Methods
Annealing defines a sequence of intermediate densities that gradually transport samples from a reference to a target, see

Annealing-based methods differ in how they navigate this sequence.
PT runs multiple Markov chains in parallel, periodically proposing swaps between adjacent chains to facilitate information exchange.

Neural Samplers amortize sampling by training neural networks to generate samples from a target distribution
(Arbel

Motivated by diffusion models, several works adapt these formulations to design new Neural Samplers

Non-amortized Methods.
This line of work aims to exploit diffusion-based ideas without resorting to neural network training.
Diffusive Gibbs Sampling (DiGS,
(Chen

,
2024
)
) introduces Gaussian convolutions to define a noisy auxiliary

Conclusions
In this work, we introduced Conditional Diffusion Sampling (CDS), a training-free framework that combines the robust global exploration of Parallel Tempering (PT) with the efficient local transport of diffusion processes.
By deriving Conditional Interpolants, we obtained exact, closed-form SDEs that transport samples from an accessible initialization distribution to the target.
Both theoretical and empirical results show that the initialization cost vanishes at short diffusion times.
Our extensive evaluation on high-dimensional benchmarks demonstrates that CDS achieves a superior trade-off between sample quality and computational cost.
