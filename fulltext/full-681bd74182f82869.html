Abstract
Reconstructing dynamic 3D scenes from sparse multi-view videos is highly ill-posed, often leading to geometric collapse, trajectory drift, and floating artifacts. Recent attempts introduce generative priors to hallucinate missing content, yet naive integration frequently causes structural drift and temporal inconsistency due to the mismatch between stochastic 2D generation and deterministic 3D geometry. In this paper, we propose GeoRect4D, a novel unified framework for sparse-view dynamic reconstruction that couples explicit 3D consistency with generative refinement via a closed-loop optimization process. Specifically, GeoRect4D introduces a degradation-aware feedback mechanism that incorporates a robust anchor-based dynamic 3DGS substrate with a single-step diffusion rectifier to hallucinate high-fidelity details. This rectifier utilizes a structural locking mechanism and spatiotemporal coordinated attention, effectively preserving physical plausibility while restoring missing content. Furthermore, we present a progressive optimization strategy that employs stochastic geometric purification to eliminate floaters and generative distillation to infuse texture details into the explicit representation. Discussion
Conclusion.
This paper presents GeoRect4D designed to tackle the highly ill-posed problem of dynamic 3D scene reconstruction from extremely sparse multi-view videos. By synergistically coupling a robust explicit dynamic 3D Gaussian Splatting substrate with a degradation-aware generative prior, our approach effectively overcomes prevalent issues such as geometric collapse, trajectory drift, and floating artifacts. Extensive evaluations demonstrate that GeoRect4D establishes a new state-of-the-art in both rende

渲染保真度和感知真实感。通过在严格保持时空一致性的同时成功幻构出细粒度细节，这项工作弥合了物理几何约束与二维生成先验之间的差距，从而实现了高质量的自由视点视频合成。
局限性。
尽管GeoRect4D很有效，但它有两个主要局限性。首先，该框架的性能仍然对基于SfM的初始化较为敏感，因为无纹理区域或极端遮挡会导致重建效果欠佳。其次，尽管单步扩散先验很高效，但与纯显式动态建模相比，闭环生成蒸馏仍然引入了更高的训练开销。
