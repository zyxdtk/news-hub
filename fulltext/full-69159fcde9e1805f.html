Abstract
Real-time strategy (RTS) games present significant AI challenges, characterized by expansive state-action spaces arising from multi-unit coordination in continuous battlefields, and sparse delayed rewards stemming from final win/lose signals. Existing approaches face a trade-off between managing the dimensionality explosion of joint actions and maintaining the interpretability of complex state representations. This complexity is further intensified by the limitation of traditional hierarchical structures

in adaptively decomposing tasks into effective tactical modules. Such difficulties are compounded by the black-box nature of deep learning models and their reliance on sparse rewards, which together result in limited sample efficiency and a lack of decision-making transparency. To address these limitations, this paper proposes HRL-IM/CBS, a hierarchical reinforcement learning framework with influence map hashing and cluster-based scripts for StarCraft micromanagement. Influence map hashing encodes global battlefield situations into compact hexadecimal codes, capturing spatial control and relative advantage. Cluster-based scripts enable dynamic local coordination through adaptive unit partitioning. The hierarchical multi-Q-table architecture decomposes decision-making into upper-level clustering strategy selection and lower-level tactical execution, with reward allocation providing dense learning signals. Experiments across six asymmetric scenarios demonstrate competitive performance against deep RL baselines while offering advantages in sample efficiency and interpretability through transparent Q-table representations.

State Space Based on Influence Map Hashing
3.2.1
Artificial Potential Field and Influence Map
In fields of robot path planning, Artificial Potential Field (APF), inspired by the interaction forces between charged particles, navigates robots by constructing virtual potential fields, which has advantages of simple design, real-time responsiveness, and high computational efficiency. Similarly, RTS games involve a large number of path planning tasks, including micromanagement. As shown in Figure
1
(a), target positions and obstacles can be represented as attractive and repulsive points respectively. Units determine their direction of movement based on the gradient of the potential field, ultimately approaching the target position and avoiding obstacles.
However, in RTS games, path planning tasks are often complicated scenarios where there may be multiple or no exact target locations. The single path provide

d by the APF may not be able to adapt to dynamic scenarios or meet actual needs. Therefore, the influence map has made improvements in this regard, which considers units as influence points that generate ripples of influence around them, divides the map into a grid map and calculates the influence value for each grid, and ultimately obtains an influence matrix, as illustrated in Figure
1
(b).
Square influence maps are used to simplify calculations and highlight enemy units’ distribution impact. Self units have positive influence values ([16,9,4,1]), while enemy units have negative ones ([-16,-9,-4,-1]). Each unit’s influence range is a 7×7 square area, with absolute influence values decreasing from 16 at the center to 1 at the edges.

Script

Conclusion
In this paper, a HRL-IM/CBS framework is proposed combined with influence map and script methods for RTS micro. To effectively reduce the size of the state and action space, an influence map hashing mechanism is designed to express the high-dimensional combat space information and cluster-based script is introduced to ensure the flexible control of global team or local units. With a hierarchical Q-learning architecture combined with combat space cl

结合聚类和奖励分配机制，战术协作可被分解为上层和下层任务。在上层，有一个Q表表示空间聚类层面的团队战术；而在下层，众多Q表表示战斗层面的单位动作规则。仅利用经典Q-learning从零开始进行训练，创建了一个轻量但具可解释性的模型，这一点已通过本研究构建的公平场景上的实验分析得到了验证。通过将训练好的模型直接部署在原始地图及其镜像地图上，所提出的方法在不同场景下展现出了有效性和广泛的适用性。
在未来的工作中，计划研究应用机器学习技术进行自动脚本提取，以取代对人工设计的依赖。此外，还将探索训练好的模型如何作为前向知识模型来支持进化计算等高级算法，从而促进高质量解的生成并为进化提供指导。与此同时，将对游戏状态数据流的实时聚类及相应的状态距离度量进行研究，从而为定义即时战略（RTS）游戏解序列之间的邻域关系铺平道路。通过这些努力，预计这一可信的、领域知识驱动的模型将在两者中发挥更大的作用

AI research and real-world multi-agent collaborative scheduling problems that share essential similarities with RTS micromanagement.
