为了释放编辑的真正潜力，我们引入了首个用于智能编辑的自动化且可扩展的数据合成流程，将多样化的视觉问答（VQA）数据转化为推理密集型的编辑指令。通过显式地嵌入问题和嵌套逻辑，我们迫使模型在执行编辑操作之前先解决潜在的问题，从而在训练过程中平衡理解与生成之间的难度。具体而言，我们从 LLaVA-OneVision-1.5 [5] 的各类别中提取问题，并将其转化为具有逻辑结构的编辑指令。例如，某条指令可能要求模型先解出一道数学方程，然后将推理过程和最终答案都写在黑板上。为了生成相应的目标图像，我们采用 Nano-Banana-Pro (Nano-Pro) [6] 作为我们的编辑主干网络。

Given the high complexity of our instructions, we supply the original VQA questions and answers as additional context to Nano-Pro to ensure editing accuracy. Finally, we utilize GPT-4o [7] to rigorously filter the generated data based on instruction following, visual aesthetics, and logical coherence. This complete pipeline ultimately yields Uni-Edit-148k, a curated dataset that pairs complex reasoning instructions with high-quality edited images across a wide range of knowledge domains. To validate the efficacy and robustness of Uni-Edit, we use it to fine-tune BAGEL, current state-of-the-art unified model. Our results demonstrate that, even without data balancing or auxiliary modules, Uni-Edit improves BAGEL’s performance across generation, editing, and understanding tasks. This finding suggests that with carefully designed editing data, image editing can serve as a general task for unified models tuning. Furthermore, we apply Uni-Edit to both auto-regressive and diffusion-based architectures, revealing distinct advantages of the BAGEL framework.
