Dead features and revival methods.
A dead feature is a dictionary element that never activates on any input.

Discussion
Feature death in SAEs is often not a training problem at all. In models with high outlier severity (
γ
\gamma
), activation geometry determines which features will live and die before the first gradient step. The two death pathways (dead-by-ReLU and dead-by-TopK) originate from the same geometric cause but recover on very different timescales. Bias learning is the bottleneck at high
γ
\gamma
, explaining why AuxK rescues some models but not others.
We did not initially expect the mechanism to be this direct. A more natural hypothesis (and the one we started with) was that outliers corrupt gradients or distort the loss landscape, causing training to slowly break down. Instead, the damage is done at initialization, and no amount of training recovers it within practical compute budgets.
