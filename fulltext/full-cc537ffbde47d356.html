5
Experiments
We evaluate QVIRL along three dimensions corresponding to the main claims of the paper. First, in randomized gridworlds where high-quality MCMC samples from the reward posterior are available, we test whether QVIRL provides an accurate and calibrated approximation to the Bayesian posterior. Second, we evaluate whether this posterior is useful for apprenticeship learning, both when extracting policies from the posterior mean and when using posterior uncertainty to derive risk-sensitive policies. Third, we test whether QVIRL can serve as a scalable Bayesian backbone for active inverse reinforcement learning, where acquisition functions require uncertainty estimates over Q-values or policies.
We conduct our experiments in three types of environments. Randomized gridworlds provide a controlled tabular setting with known transition dynamics, allowing us to compare variational posterior approximations to ValueWalk
(
4
)
, a MCMC-based method able to sample from the true posterior,

which we use as a reference oracle. Lunar Lander and Highway Env provide larger continuous-state benchmarks for evaluating apprenticeship learning performance, stability, and data efficiency from compact vector-based observations. Finally, we run apprenticeship learning on ATARI games with raw-pixel observations to demonstrate the scaling potential of our method. We provide further details on the environments, expert policies, and demonstration collection procedure in Appendix

We compare QVIRL against three baselines. AVRIL

6
讨论
在上一节中，我们根据贝叶斯IRL方法的期望特性评估了QVIRL。
首先，QVIRL旨在通过变分推断近似最优Q值和奖励的真实贝叶斯后验。尽管依赖于可处理的近似

，我们在第5节的实验结果表明，QVIRL在表格环境中能恢复校准良好的后验分布，而AVRIL无法近似真实的奖励后验，仅提供最优Q值的点估计。基于MCMC的贝叶斯IRL方法（如ValueWalk和PolicyWalk）虽然也能估计Q值和奖励的真实后验，但难以扩展到小型环境之外。相比之下，QVIRL利用变分推断将原则性的后验估计扩展到更复杂的环境。

其次，QVIRL在从专家演示中进行学徒学习时表现出色。这是一种高效的基于梯度的算法，与大多数贝叶斯IRL方法不同，它甚至支持从原始像素观测中进行训练。

最后，QVIRL自然支持主动学习。通过显式建模最优Q值后验，QVIRL可作为可扩展的贝叶斯IRL主干，用于追踪Q值空间不确定性的采集函数。这消除了以往基于MCMC的主动IRL方法的关键可扩展性瓶颈，为在更大环境中实现主动学习提供了可能。

7
结论
我们提出了一种可扩展的贝叶斯逆强化学习方法，能够保持对最优Q值和奖励的后验不确定性量化。据我们所知，QVIRL是首个显式建模联合后验的贝叶斯IRL变分方法

在扩展到小型表格环境之外时，获得最优 Q 值。该算法不仅在与贝叶斯逆向强化学习（IRL）基线的对比中表现出色，而且在与最先进的模仿学习方法的对比中也具有竞争力。由于对最优 Q 值进行了有原则的不确定性量化，QVIRL 在设计上支持可扩展的主动学习。这些贡献是向将具有校准不确定性的贝叶斯 IRL 方法引入高维环境迈出的重要一步。
