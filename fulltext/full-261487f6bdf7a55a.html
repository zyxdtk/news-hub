原标题：Which Nash Equilibrium? Solver-Dependent Selection on Zero-Sum Nash Polytopes

中文摘要： 本文研究在存在多个纳什均衡的两人零和博弈中，不同求解算法是否会系统性地选择不同均衡。实验表明，均衡选择由算法而非随机种子决定，且差异仅在几何不对称的纳什集中显现。正则化末次迭代方法（如R-NaD）倾向于选择最大熵均衡，而遗憾平均方法（如CFR+）偏向较低熵的边界。所选均衡对次优对手的下游影响有限。研究还纠正了两个直觉：CFR的边界漂移并非由正象限投影引起，且R-NaD的选择依赖于初始化锚点。

Introduction
A central simplification in computational game theory is the treatment of a Nash equilibrium as a unique target: one solves a game, obtains
the

Scope and resources.
This is an independent research project carried out on a single personal computer (commodity CPU, no GPU or compute cluster). Several of the scoping choices above are therefore as much practical as principled: the tabular, exactly solvable testbed was chosen both because it admits analytic ground truth
and
because it runs end-to-end on a laptop in minutes; the absence of function-approximation and sampling (e.g. Monte-Carlo CFR or deep) experiments, and the restriction of the random ensemble to matrix games with a single extensive-form instance, reflect this compute budget rather than a belief that the phenomena vanish at scale. We have tried to make the constraint a virtue—every number and figure is reproducible from exact counterfactual values on modest hardware—and we flag scaling these experiments to function-approximation regimes and larger extensive-form games as the natural, resource-permitting extension.
