# Lovable在iOS和Android上推出氛围编程应用 [Sarah Perez](https://techcrunch.com/author/sarah-perez/) 太平洋夏令时间上午9:09 · 2026年4月28日 [](https://www.facebook.com/sharer.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Ftechcrunch.com%2F2026%2F04%2F28%2Flovable-launches-its-vibe-coding-app-on-ios-and-android%2F)[](https://twitter.com/intent/tweet?url=https%3A%2F%2Ftechcrunch.com%2F2026%2F04%2F28%2Flovable-launches-its-vibe-coding-app-on-ios-and-android%2F&amp;text=Lovable+launches+its+vibe-coding+app+on+iOS+and+Android&amp;via=techcrunch)[](https://www.linkedin.com/shareArticle?url=https%3A%2F%2Ftechcrunch.com%2F2026%2F04%2F28%2Flovable-launches-its-vibe-coding-app-on-ios-and-android%2F&amp;title=Lovable+launches+its+vibe-coding+app+on+iOS+and+Android&amp;summary=The+app+allows+developers+to+vibe+code+web+apps+and+websites+on+the+go.+&amp;mini=1&amp;source=TechCrunch)[](https://www.reddit.com/submit?url=https%3A%2F%2Ftechcrunch.com%2F2026%2F04%2F28%2Flovable-launches-its-vibe-coding-app-on-ios-and-android%2F&amp;title=Lovable+launches+its+vibe-coding+app+on+iOS+and+Android)[](mailto:?subject=Lovable+launches+its+vibe-coding+app+on+iOS+and+Android&amp;body=Article%3A+https%3A%2F%2Ftechcrunch.com%2F2026%2F04%2F28%2Flovable-launches-its-vibe-coding-app-on-ios-and-android%2F)[](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/28/lovable-launches-its-vibe-coding-app-on-ios-and-android/) 苹果最近对氛围编程应用的[打击](https://9to5mac.com/2026/03/30/apple-steps-up-crackdown-on-vibe-coding-apps-pulls-anything-from-the-app-store/)并没有阻碍Lovable推出其无代码AI应用构建器，该应用[现已作为](https://lovable.dev/blog/mobile-app)移动应用在[苹果](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/lovable-build-with-ai/id6757471107)和[谷歌](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=dev.lovable.build)的应用商店上架。

这家氛围编程初创公司的新移动应用向潜在的应用构建者推销，作为一种通过语音或文本AI提示在移动中进行编程的方式，让你在灵感闪现时就能捕捉想法。这意味着你可以在任何地方启动Lovable来处理你随机的应用想法，让它的智能体在接收你的输入后自主运行。

新应用还允许你在电脑和手机之间来回切换，从你在一个特定项目上停下的地方继续，并在构建准备好审查时接收通知。

该应用的推出是在苹果明确了氛围编程应用在其App Store上能做什么和不能做什么之后不久。这家科技巨头[最近阻止了](https://www.cnet.com/tech/services-and-software/apple-app-store-guidelines-have-some-vibe-coding-apps-in-limbo/)对流行氛围编程工具（包括Replit和Vibecode）的更新，原因是违反了其开发者指南。

简而言之，苹果并没有封杀氛围编程应用本身，但它不允许应用下载新代码或改变其功能，因为这会对最终用户造成安全风险。（这也意味着苹果的App Review团队在审批过程中无法对应用进行适当的审查。）

苹果还因类似原因暂时将氛围编程应用Anything从App Store[下架](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/03/30/apple-pulls-vibe-coding-app/)，但该应用在[本月早些时候](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/14/how-vibe-coding-app-anything-is-rebuilding-after-getting-booted-from-the-app-store-twice/)进行修改后又[重新上架](https://x.com/anything/status/2040172250163237192)。

为了遵守苹果的规定，氛围编程应用不再能够在宿主应用内运行其生成的应用。相反，这些应用预览被转移到了网络浏览器中。

Lovable似乎也遵守了这些规则，因为其新应用宣扬能够将想法转化为“可运行的网站或网络应用”。
