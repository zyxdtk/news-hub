5 结论
我们提出了 Cortex，据我们所知，这是第一个通过本体语料库图（OCG）统一内容质量精炼、轻量级本体构建和跨域关联建模的 Web 规模语料库构建框架。实验验证了精炼流水线、OCG 的领域组织和跨域关联建模以及数据合成方法的有效性。
我们公开发布了完整的流水线、一个包含 24.14 B token 的精炼中文语料库及其 OCG，以及 CortexBench。
通过将质量精炼与结构化知识组织相结合，Cortex 将语料库构建推向了超越扁平文档过滤的阶段，在 Web 规模上建立了一个高质量、组织良好的本体语料库图。

局限性
当前的实例化处理单个 Common Crawl 快照并在中文上进行了验证；CortexBench 的数据合成仅使用了 OCG 超过 130 万个关键词中的 42 个。这些范围选择源于计算限制而非方法论限制：流水线的每个阶段在不同快照上独立运行，且数据合成过程可以轻松扩展到完整的关键词词汇表。这使得扩展到更多快照成为可能

evaluation and training.

Cross-Setting Performance Progression.
Across all eight models, the four evaluation settings reveal a
consistent progression pattern. In the
Closed-book
setting,
bridge questions (avg 33.0%) prove slightly more accessible than
comparison questions (avg 26.4%), likely because bridge questions
sometimes permit partial answers from parametric knowledge about
individual entities. The
1-Search
setting produces highly
variable improvements: Gemini 3.1 Pro achieves the largest gain on
bridge (+37.6 points to 65.7%), while GPT-4o gains only 0.3 points,
suggesting that sing

dramatically across model families. The
Multi-Search

benefits models with strong iterative planning (Gemini 3 Pro gains
24.9 points from 1-Search to reach 63.0% on bridge), but notably
degrades Gemini 3.1 Pro by 13.2 points on bridge and 13.7 on
comparison, indicating that additional search rounds can introduce
noise when the initial retrieval was already highly effective.
Evidence Retrieval Analysis.
Table
17
reveals a strong correspondence between
evidence hit rates and accuracy. Gemini 3.1 Pro achieves the highest
Hit@5 (38.4% bridge, 46.0% comparison) but the lowest Hit@15
(14.1%, 12.6%), explaining its high 1-Search accuracy but
Multi-Search degradation: its initial queries are highly effective,
but subsequent queries fail to locate additional evidence and may
introduce distracting content. Conversely, Gemini 3 Pro shows the
opposite pattern with low Hit@5 (22.5%, 24.6%) but the highest
Hit@15 (55.0%, 50.9%), explaining its large Multi-Search gains.
GPT-4o achieves relatively high Hit@5 (33.4%, 36.6%) but
translates this poorly into accuracy gains (+0.3 on bridge), suggesting
a reasoning bottleneck rather than a retrieval bottleneck.
